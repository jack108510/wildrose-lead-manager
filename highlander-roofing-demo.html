Our Services

NR

New Roof Installation

Full roof systems for new builds and major renovations. We work with asphalt shingles, metal roofing, and synthetic slate to match any architectural style.

RF

Roof Replacement

When repairs aren't enough, we provide complete tear-off and replacement. Honest assessments and transparent pricing with no hidden surprises.

RP

Leak & Storm Repair

Fast, reliable repairs for hail damage, wind damage, and persistent leaks. We work with your insurance company to make the process seamless.

MT

Preventive Maintenance

Annual inspections and maintenance plans to extend the life of your roof. Small problems caught early save thousands down the road.

VT

Ventilation & Insulation

Proper attic ventilation and insulation are critical in Calgary's climate. We assess and upgrade to prevent ice dams and reduce energy costs.

FL

Flat Roof Systems

Specialized solutions for flat and low-slope roofs including TPO, modified bitumen, and rubber EPDM systems for garages, additions, and commercial spaces.
