About Qadr Design & Print

Based in Edmonton, Alberta, Qadr Design & Print is a creative studio dedicated to helping businesses stand out through exceptional design and print work.

We combine artistic vision with technical precision to deliver materials that look stunning and perform even better. Whether you need a single business card or a full brand overhaul, we approach every project with the same commitment to quality.

Our team works closely with each client to understand their goals, ensuring every piece we create is purposeful, polished, and on-brand.
